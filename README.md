# Secret Chat

A simple private chat app for two people at a time. Add a friend with their personal code, chat, share files, and wipe the whole conversation whenever you want.

## Features

- **Personal code.** You get an 8-character code. Share it so friends can add you.
- **Contact requests.** Nobody can chat with you until you accept their request.
- **Private rooms.** Each chat is visible only to you and that one contact.
- **Messages and files.** Send text and files up to 5 MB. Tap a received file to save it.
- **Wipe anytime.** Removing a contact deletes all your messages and files with them, for both of you.

## Install (Android)

1. Open the **Releases** page of this repository.
2. Download the `.apk` file from the latest release.
3. Open it on your phone. If asked, allow installing apps from this source.

Because the app isn't from the Play Store, your phone may show a warning. Tap **Install anyway**.

## How to use

1. Create an account with your email and a password.
2. Your code is at the top of the home screen. Tap it to copy and share it.
3. To add someone, tap **Add contact** and enter their code.
4. When they accept, they appear in your contact list. Tap them to chat.
5. Tap the paperclip to send a file.
6. Tap the red trash icon to remove a contact and wipe the chat.

## Good to know

- **Messages are not end-to-end encrypted yet.** Other users can't read your chats, but the app's server owner can. Don't send anything highly sensitive.
- Your contacts can see your email address.
- Files are limited to 5 MB each.
- Wiped chats can't be recovered.
